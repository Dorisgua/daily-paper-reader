## Abstract
Electroencephalography (EEG) and magnetoencephalography (MEG) measure neural activity non-invasively by capturing electromagnetic fields generated by dendritic currents. 
Although rooted in the same biophysics, EEG and MEG exhibit distinct signal patterns, further complicated by variations in sensor configurations across modalities and recording devices.
Existing approaches typically rely on separate, modality- and dataset-specific models, which limits the performance and cross-domain scalability.
This paper proposes BrainOmni, the first brain foundation model that generalises across heterogeneous EEG and MEG recordings. 
To unify diverse data sources, we introduce BrainTokenizer,  the first tokeniser that quantises spatiotemporal brain activity into discrete representations. 
Central to BrainTokenizer is a novel Sensor Encoder that encodes sensor properties such as spatial layout, orientation, and type, enabling compatibility across devices and modalities.
Building upon the discrete representations, BrainOmni learns unified semantic embeddings of brain signals by self-supervised pretraining. To the best of our knowledge, it is the first foundation model to support both EEG and MEG signals, as well as the first to incorporate large-scale MEG pretraining.
A total of 1,997 hours of EEG and 656 hours of MEG data are curated and standardised from publicly available sources for pretraining.
Experiments show that BrainOmni outperforms both existing foundation models and state-of-the-art task-specific models on a range of downstream tasks. It also demonstrates strong generalisation to unseen EEG and MEG devices. Further analysis reveals that joint EEG-MEG (EMEG) training yields consistent improvements across both modalities. Code and checkpoints are publicly available at https://github.com/OpenTSLab/BrainOmni

### 2. 论文提出的方法论

- **核心思想：** 采用两阶段训练策略。第一阶段（BrainTokenizer）将异质的原始EMEG信号统一量化为离散化的“潜在源变量”token；第二阶段（BrainOmni）基于这些token进行自监督预训练，学习统一的语义表示。

- **关键技术细节：**
    - **第一阶段：BrainTokenizer (标记化)**
        - **输入：** 样本表示为 Ω = (X, L, S)，其中X是原始信号 (C×T)，L是传感器3D位置和方向 (C×6)，S是传感器类型 (EEG/GRAD/MAG)。
        - **SEANet编码器：** 使用1D卷积神经网络提取信号的**时间**特征 Z_time。
        - **传感器编码器：** **核心创新**。通过位置嵌入层和类型嵌入层，将传感器物理特性L和S编码为传感器嵌入V。这使得模型不依赖于固定的通道命名惯例，能兼容任意设备。
        - **交叉注意力与通道压缩：** 可学习的Query与（Z_time + V）进行交叉注意力，输出固定数量的**潜在源变量** Z_src。这个过程相当于将可变数量的通道映射到统一的潜在特征空间，模拟了经典的源活动估计中的“逆问题”求解。
        - **残差向量量化 (RVQ)：** 使用4层RVQ将Z_src离散化为离散token Q。
        - **重构器：** 反向过程，使用交叉注意力和SEANet解码器从离散token重建原始信号X_hat，模拟源估计的“正问题”。
        - **训练损失：** 联合优化多项损失：时域L1损失（`L_time`）、频域幅度/相位损失（`L_freq`）、皮尔逊相关系数损失（`L_pcc`）和RVQ承诺损失（`L_rvq`）。其中`L_freq`和`L_pcc`分别用于保留频谱细节和抑制伪影。

    - **第二阶段：BrainOmni (自监督预训练)**
        - **输入：** BrainTokenizer生成的token序列。
        - **掩码预测：** 随机掩码50%的token位置，然后通过一个跨通道-时间的**十字交叉Transformer**来预测被掩码的token索引。
        - **训练损失：** 对每个掩码token的所有RVQ层的预测计算交叉熵损失（`L_model`）。

### 3. 实验设计

- **下游任务与数据集：** 在**11个数据集**、9个任务上进行了评估，涵盖：

    - **MEG任务：** 自闭症谱系障碍（ASD74）、抑郁症（MEG-MMI）。
